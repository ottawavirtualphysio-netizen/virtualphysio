Common questions

Do you offer in-home physiotherapy in Barrhaven?

Yes. We visit homes in Barrhaven and nearby neighbourhoods. Choose "In my home" when you book and tell us your area, and we'll confirm the visit.

Can I do virtual physiotherapy from Barrhaven?

Yes. Virtual sessions work from anywhere in Ontario. You just need a phone, tablet or computer with a camera.

Do I need a referral?

No. In Ontario you can book directly with a physiotherapist. Some insurance plans ask for a doctor's note before they reimburse.

Using your insurance

Many extended health plans cover physiotherapy by a registered physiotherapist, and many include virtual and in-home visits. Car accident injuries may be covered by your auto insurance accident benefits.

Tell us your insurer when you book. Our guide explains referrals, OHIP and insurance in Ontario.

FAQ

Common questions

What conditions can virtual physiotherapy treat?

Back and neck pain, joint stiffness and arthritis, tendon problems, sports injuries, post-surgery rehab, pelvic floor concerns, balance problems and general movement limitations.

How many sessions will I need?

It depends on your condition and goals. After your assessment your physiotherapist will recommend a plan. Many people start weekly and space sessions out as they improve.

Can virtual physiotherapy help after surgery?

Yes. Once the early phase is over, virtual sessions work well for exercise progression, education and monitoring. In-home visits are available when hands-on care is needed.

Do you offer in-home physiotherapy?

Yes. We visit homes across Ottawa and nearby communities. Share your location and preferred times when booking.

Do I need a referral?

No. In Ontario you can book directly with a physiotherapist. Some insurance plans require a doctor's note for reimbursement.

What should I wear?

Comfortable clothing that lets the area being treated be seen and moved, for example shorts for knee problems or a tank top for shoulder problems.

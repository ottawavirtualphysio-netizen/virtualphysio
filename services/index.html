Insurance

Using your insurance

Many extended health plans cover physiotherapy by a registered physiotherapist, and many include virtual and in-home visits. Car accident injuries may be covered through your auto insurance accident benefits.

FAQ

Common questions

How many sessions will I need?

It depends on your condition and goals. After your assessment your physiotherapist will recommend a plan. Many people start weekly and space sessions out as they improve.

Virtual, in-home or home clinic room: which should I choose?

Virtual works well for exercise-based care, education and follow-ups. In-home is best when hands-on treatment is needed or travel is hard. The home clinic room offers in-person care by request. Not sure? Book a free intro call and we'll explain the options.

Do I need a referral?

No. In Ontario you can book directly with a physiotherapist. Some insurance plans require a doctor's note for reimbursement.

Is physiotherapy covered by insurance?

Many extended health plans cover physiotherapy by a registered physiotherapist. Car accident injuries may be covered through auto insurance. Tell us your insurer when you book.

What should I wear?

Comfortable clothing that lets the area being treated be seen and moved, for example shorts for a knee problem.

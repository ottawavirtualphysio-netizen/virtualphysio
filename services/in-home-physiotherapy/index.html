In-Home Physiotherapy in Ottawa

Your physiotherapist comes to you. Hands-on treatment and exercise built around your real home: your stairs, your chair, your space.

Who it's for

Can this help you?

  • Recovery after surgery or a fracture
  • Older adults, balance problems and falls prevention
  • Anyone who can't drive or finds travel painful
  • Chronic pain or long-term conditions with limited energy
  • New parents who'd rather not travel
  • People who want care in a private, familiar setting

What to expect

How it works

Booking

Tell us your area and preferred times. We'll confirm the visit and any parking or building access details.

At your home

A full assessment and hands-on treatment. We'll check how you manage stairs, transfers and daily tasks, and look for fall hazards.

Your plan

Exercises designed for your space and equipment, plus a clear plan for follow-up visits, which can be in-home or virtual.

FAQ

Common questions

Which areas do you visit?

Ottawa and nearby communities, including Kanata, Stittsville, Barrhaven, Nepean, Orléans, Gloucester, Westboro, Centretown, Alta Vista, Riverside South, Findlay Creek and Manotick. Ask us if you're not sure.

Do I need any equipment?

No. We'll work with what you have, such as a sturdy chair, a step or a wall.

How long is a home visit?

The first visit is usually about an hour. Follow-ups are typically 30 to 45 minutes.

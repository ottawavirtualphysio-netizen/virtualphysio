How it works

In-home visits in the first weeks, when travel is hardest: swelling control, safe movement, walking aids and transfers.

Building strength

Progressive exercises matched to your stage of healing and your surgeon's protocol.

Back to normal life

FAQ

Common questions

When should I start physiotherapy after surgery?

Often within days, following your surgeon's instructions. Many people continue rehab at home after discharge.

How long does rehab take?

It depends on the procedure. Most people see major gains over 6 to 12 weeks, and full recovery can take 6 to 12 months.

Can I combine in-home and virtual sessions?

Yes. Many people start in-home and switch to virtual follow-ups as they become more independent.

FAQ

Common questions

Is physiotherapy covered after a car accident in Ontario?

Usually, through your own auto insurance accident benefits and any extended health benefits. Contact your insurer to open a claim.

What is the Minor Injury Guideline?

It's the framework under Ontario's accident benefits rules for injuries like sprains, strains and whiplash. It caps medical and rehab benefits for those injuries at $3,500.

How soon should I start?

As soon as serious injuries have been ruled out. Early, gentle activity supports recovery from whiplash.

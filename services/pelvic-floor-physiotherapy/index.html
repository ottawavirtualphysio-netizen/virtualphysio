Pelvic Floor Physiotherapy in Ottawa

Bladder leaks, pelvic pain and heaviness are common, but you don't have to live with them. Pelvic floor muscle training is a recommended first-line treatment for many of these problems.

Book pelvic floor physiotherapy Call +1 (343) 202-4226
Who it's for

Can this help you?

  • Leaking when you cough, sneeze, laugh or exercise
  • Urgency or needing to pee often
  • Heaviness or bulging (prolapse symptoms)
  • Pelvic, tailbone or pain with intercourse
  • Pregnancy-related pelvic and back pain
  • Postpartum recovery and diastasis recti

What to expect

How it works

A private conversation

We talk through your symptoms, bladder and bowel habits, pregnancies and goals, at your pace.

Assessment, with your consent

Posture, breathing, abdominal wall, core and hips. Every part is explained first, and you can decline or stop anything at any time.

Treatment

Strengthening or relaxation training, bladder strategies and a plan to return to running, lifting or sport.

FAQ

Common questions

Is the assessment private?

Yes. Sessions are one-on-one, every step is explained first, and nothing is done without your consent. You can bring a support person if you like.

Can pelvic floor physiotherapy be done virtually?

Much of it can, including education, breathing and muscle training, bladder strategies and core rehab. It's also a comfortable first step.

When can I start after giving birth?

Gentle breathing and pelvic floor exercises can often begin within days. A postpartum check is commonly booked around six weeks after birth.

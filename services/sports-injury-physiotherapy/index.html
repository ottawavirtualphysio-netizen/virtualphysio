FAQ

Common questions

How soon can I return to sport?

It depends on the injury. Returning based on strength and balance tests, rather than time alone, lowers the risk of re-injury.

Can sports injuries be treated virtually?

Many can. Exercise-based rehab and return-to-run planning work well online. We'll recommend in-person care if hands-on testing is needed.

Do I need an X-ray or MRI first?

Usually not. Your physiotherapist will refer you for imaging or a medical review if the assessment suggests it's needed.

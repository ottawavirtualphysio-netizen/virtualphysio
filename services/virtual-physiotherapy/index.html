What to expect

How it works

Before your session

You'll get a secure video link. Set up a phone, tablet or laptop with a little floor space around you.

Assessment

We talk through your history and goals, then your physiotherapist guides you through movement and strength tests on camera.

Treatment and plan

You'll learn your exercises live, with corrections as you go, and receive a written home program and a follow-up plan.

FAQ

Common questions

Is virtual physiotherapy effective?

For many conditions where exercise and education are the main treatment, research reviews suggest results can be comparable to in-person care. If you need hands-on treatment, we'll arrange an in-home visit.

What do I need?

A device with a camera, a stable internet connection, comfortable clothing and a little floor space. A chair and a wall are helpful.

Is it covered by insurance?

Many extended health plans cover virtual physiotherapy by a registered physiotherapist. Check your plan or call your insurer to confirm.

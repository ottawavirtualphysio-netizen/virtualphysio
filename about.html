About Virtual Physio

Physiotherapy built around you, not the clinic schedule

Virtual Physio offers one-on-one physiotherapy online and in homes across Ottawa, so getting good care doesn't depend on getting to a clinic.

Our approach

Listen first, then build a plan that works in real life

Every appointment begins with a focused assessment and a conversation about what matters to you. From there we set clear goals and build a plan that respects your lifestyle, comfort and pace.

Treatment may include exercise, hands-on techniques, posture and movement training, education and self-management strategies. Each step is explained in plain language, and you're encouraged to ask questions at any point.

The goal isn't just to relieve pain today. It's to help you move with confidence and independence, and to know what to do if symptoms come back.

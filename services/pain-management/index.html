FAQ

Common questions

Do I need a scan before physiotherapy?

Usually not. Most back and neck pain can be assessed and treated without imaging. We'll refer you if anything suggests you need one.

How many sessions will I need?

It varies from person to person. Your physiotherapist will recommend a plan after your assessment. Long-standing pain often needs a longer plan, mostly built around exercise you do at home.

Is exercise safe when I'm in pain?

Exercise at the right dose is a recommended treatment for many types of persistent pain, including chronic low back pain. We'll start where you're comfortable and adjust as you go.

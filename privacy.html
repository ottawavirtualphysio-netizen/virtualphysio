Privacy policy

Last updated November 25, 2026

Virtual Physio respects your privacy and protects your personal health information in line with Ontario's Personal Health Information Protection Act (PHIPA) and the standards of the College of Physiotherapists of Ontario.

What we collect

  • Booking requests: your name, phone number, email (optional), age group, the service and visit type you choose, your area, preferred times, insurance provider and anything you choose to tell us about your symptoms.
  • Clinical records: if you become a patient, the assessment and treatment notes physiotherapists are required to keep.
  • Website data: basic technical information such as your browser and IP address, used to keep the website secure and prevent spam.

Why we collect it

To respond to your request, schedule and provide care, communicate with you, bill you or your insurer when you ask us to, and meet our legal and professional obligations. We don't sell your information or use it for advertising.

How it's stored and shared

  • Booking requests sent through this website are stored by our website host, Netlify.
  • If you choose to message us on WhatsApp, that conversation is handled by WhatsApp (Meta) under its own privacy policy.
  • We share health information only with your consent, or where the law requires or permits it.
  • Clinical records are kept securely for as long as professional regulations require.

Your choices and rights

You can ask to see or correct your personal health information, withdraw your consent to being contacted, or ask us to delete a booking request that didn't lead to care. Please don't include more health detail in the booking form than you're comfortable sharing. We'll ask what we need when we speak with you.

Contact us

Questions or requests about your privacy: info@virtualphysio.ca or +1 (343) 202-4226. If you have concerns we can't resolve, you can contact the Information and Privacy Commissioner of Ontario.
